## City Learner

[![City Learner app showing a Toronto map with a highlighted public artwork](/assets/images/city-learner.png)](https://andywaite.com/city-learner/)

City Learner is a simple game for learning about the geography of a new city. It's a single-page TypeScript app built with Vite. The map is rendered with [MapLibre GL JS](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/), progress is stored locally in IndexedDB via [Dexie](https://dexie.org/), spaced repetition uses [FSRS](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs), and the deck of city features is generated from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) data.
